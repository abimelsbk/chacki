// features

Small screen. Lots of soul.

Not a clock with eyes. Chacki has its own state, schedule and opinions.

12 animated moods

Procedural neon eyes that morph smoothly, blink, glance around, breathe and squash when you tap them.

Pet needs

Food, energy and fun drop over time. Feed it data cubes, play ball, pet it. Neglect it and it sulks.

Personalities

Cheerful, sarcastic, sleepy or hacker. Each one has its own lines, quirks, voice pitch, and yes, matrix rain.

Skins

7 built-in themes, or drop your own .txt skin on the SD card: colours, eye shape, scanlines.

It hears you

Clap and it jumps. Double-clap to wake it up. Talk nearby and it gets curious. Hold the screen for push-to-talk.

Focus buddy

Built-in Pomodoro with a determined face, a countdown and a celebration when you finish a session.

Reminders & alarm

Drink water, stand up, your own custom reminder, a daily alarm with snooze, and a night mode that says good night.

Phone web UI

Every setting, mood and action from your browser. Password protected, served by the pet itself.

Wireless updates

Upload new firmware from the browser. Under M5 Launcher it stages safely through the SD card, so it never bricks.

// control panel

Your pet, from your pocket

Chacki hosts its own control panel. Swipe up on the device to see its address and login, then open it on any phone on the same Wi-Fi.

  • Live status: mood, needs, battery, Wi-Fi, focus timer
  • Look & feel: skins, colour pickers, eye shape, brightness
  • Behaviour: personality, clap sensitivity with a live mic meter
  • Schedule: night mode, alarm, water / stand / custom reminders
  • Network: up to 5 Wi-Fi networks; passwords are never sent back to the browser
  • Firmware: upload updates over Wi-Fi, reboot, return to Launcher
// architecture

Body on the desk, brain on the network

// install

Up and running in 4 steps

Download

Get Chacki-…-launcher.bin and the SD-card zip from the latest release.

Copy to SD

Put the .bin on the card. Unzip the SD pack to the card root and rename config.example.txt to config.txt.

Add Wi-Fi

Fill in up to 5 networks (2.4 GHz). Chacki joins whichever one is in range.

Install

In M5 Launcher: SD → Chacki .bin → Install. Swipe up to find the web UI.
