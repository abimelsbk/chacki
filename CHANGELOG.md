# Changelog

## v0.2.0 — 2026-11-19

First public release.

- Procedural neon face with 12 moods, blinking, glancing, breathing, gestures
- Pet needs (food / energy / fun) with feeding, ball play and petting
- Personalities: cheerful, sarcastic, sleepy, hacker
- 7 built-in skins + custom skins from the SD card
- Clap / double-clap / voice reactions
- Pomodoro focus buddy, water/stand/custom reminders, daily alarm, night mode
- Phone web UI (all settings, pet actions, moods)
- Wireless updates: direct OTA, or SD-staged under M5 Launcher
- Up to 5 saved Wi-Fi networks, NTP time kept in the RTC
- Push-to-talk with robot-voice echo (AI backend coming in Phase 3)
